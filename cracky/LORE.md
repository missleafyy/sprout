# Cracky (Pretend Co.) → Pokémon-style game: lore notes

Condensed from github.com/crackytests/cursed (ref/*.md, design docs). Source of truth is that repo; this is the cheat sheet.

## Tone
Deadpan, absurd, meta. Cosmic plans end in petty permissions or ad breaks; characters take silly premises seriously. Sincere and sad moments are allowed to land.
Don't: settle Carl's name or parents (always "STILL IN TRANSIT"), make Carl a cool quippy rogue (that's HU-MAN, the villain), write Linda as a generic evil AI, make Yoko a prop, do Dyslexio misspelling gags, put TP in the lore.
Soften for this game: bongs become a harmless thrown item; skip the Pee-wee Kid classroom material.

## Cast (look → role here)

- **Boo**: Leafy's ghost co-host (light-green tea body, darker green bow). → **Carl's partner**: follows him and runs the bottom screen. Not from the repo; Leafy asked to keep Boo.

- **Spooky Ghost**: white sheet ghost, black oval eyes, fanged grin, purple/cyan glitch glow. Showboat magician, owes spooky alimony. → gym leader (curse).
- **Old Face**: dark wireframe head, magenta almond eyes with green waveform pupils, え nose, always four-shade green. Freed Carl. → professor figure.
- **New Face**: glossy black masked cowl, green third-eye dot, magenta eyes, え nose.
- **Pilot X**: spiky black hair, red headband, yellow ring goggles, 天 on his nose, pale teal coat. "spoilers." Calls Carl "little brother".
- **Yoko**: brown bob, lavender sleeveless turtleneck, gold pendant. "Helping you doesn't mean doing what you say." Variants: Empress, Warworld, Yokoids.
- **JB Garfield**: fat orange noir cat, grey fedora, tan trenchcoat. Runs the Litter Box bar. Lasagna is a food group.
- **Pee Kid**: bowl cut, white sailor shirt, blue collar, brown neckerchief. Never attacks; ASK / PERFORM / INSPECT.
- **HU-MAN**: focus-tested square-jawed "normal human" Carl in sunglasses. "BY THE POWER OF NEVADA!" → **rival**.

- Others: Mall Cop ("UP ONLY! CAN'T YOU READ?"), Mimi the Gatebox waifu (copies your last move), Bruce the shark, the Gull King, Pretzel Guy, Garf of Garf's Diner.
